*Some houses keep their secrets. This one keeps you.*

## What if the system designed to predict violence became part of the danger?

Anne Rossier and Ethan Blackthorn find themselves confronting concealed histories, competing loyalties and the reach of the Circle. A system intended to anticipate violence becomes entangled with the very threat it is supposed to help control.

As the danger grows, information alone is not enough. Every revelation raises a harder question: whom can you trust with what you know—and what will they do with it?

## The cost of knowing

*The Secrets We Keep* is a conspiracy thriller about power, prediction and moral restraint. Its characters face pressure to act, but the ability to use power does not automatically make its use right. Trust becomes a decision with consequences, and the past refuses to remain safely in the past.

The tension comes not only from discovering what has been hidden, but from deciding what should happen once the truth is exposed.

## Read it if you enjoy

- Conspiracy thrillers with consequences beyond a private secret
- Characters forced to judge whom they can trust
- Systems of surveillance, prediction and control
- Buried histories and revelations that change the stakes
- Moral choices made under pressure

## The central question

**A secret can protect you. It can also make you a target—and give someone else power over your future.**
