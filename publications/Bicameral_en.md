## How much do you really know about the voice you call “I”?

An investigation into consciousness leads to an ancient chamber in Malta, unusual acoustic effects and unsettling questions about the experience of hearing an inner voice.

What begins as a scientific inquiry develops into a mystery where evidence, interpretation and ambition collide. The discovery may change how the investigators understand the mind—but it may also attract people who want to turn that knowledge into power.

## Where neuroscience meets an archaeological mystery

*Bicameral* is an intellectual thriller built around a difficult question: how do we experience ourselves as a unified self? It draws on real scientific questions about cognition and internally generated voices, while using fictional characters and events to create suspense.

The novel does not claim to prove a theory of consciousness. Instead, it explores the uncertain boundary between evidence and interpretation—and the consequences when people become convinced they know what the evidence means.

## Read it if you enjoy

- Thrillers driven by ideas as well as suspense
- Neuroscience, psychology and the puzzle of consciousness
- Ancient sites and archaeological mysteries
- Investigations where interpretation can be as consequential as evidence
- Fiction that challenges assumptions about the self

## The central question

**The most unsettling mystery may not be what you hear—but why you believe it is you speaking.**
