*Some emotions were never meant to be silenced.*

## What happens when a society that has restricted emotion begins to feel again?

For decades, emotion has been suppressed and regulated. Serina Dalvin and Simon Drell become part of a transformation that reaches beyond individual liberation: awareness begins to spread, connecting minds in ways no one fully understands.

The return of feeling brings connection, discovery and new possibilities. It also threatens the boundaries that make private thought and individual identity possible.

## Freedom has consequences

*The Choir of Dust* begins with a society that limits what people are allowed to feel, but its deeper question is what happens after those limits break. When emotions are shared, does empathy deepen—or does privacy become impossible? When a collective awareness emerges, what protects the individual?

This is not a simple story of liberation leading to a perfect world. Every transformation creates new tensions, and a solution can bring dangers its creators never anticipated.

## A novel for readers interested in

- Dystopian societies and systems of social control
- Emotional repression, liberation and resistance
- Collective consciousness and the boundaries of the self
- Ethical dilemmas without easy answers
- Speculative fiction that questions whether utopia is ever simple

## The central question

**If humanity could share its emotions, would we become more connected—or lose the solitude that makes us individuals?**
