*An ancient code. A hidden world. A truth that could change everything.*

## What if reading a text were not a neutral act?

A monk dies under unexplained circumstances. A fragment of parchment emerges, carrying questions that cannot be answered by reading its visible words alone. As the investigation develops, hidden writing and competing interpretations draw the reader into a mystery in which knowledge is never separate from power.

The clues do more than point towards a solution. They challenge the assumptions of the people trying to understand them—and the meaning of the act of reading itself.

## A mystery about the reader, not just the text

*The 14th Page* combines the appeal of a manuscript mystery with a more unsettling question: who controls the meaning of what is written? A fragment can preserve knowledge, conceal it, or become a tool in the hands of those who want to direct how others think.

Its mystery unfolds through misinterpretation, coercion, inheritance and the possibility of release. The closer the reader gets to an answer, the more complicated the act of understanding becomes.

## Read it if you enjoy

- Ancient manuscripts, hidden writing and scholarly puzzles
- Mysteries in which clues change meaning as the story develops
- Questions about knowledge, interpretation and control
- Philosophical suspense that goes beyond finding a culprit
- Stories that make the act of reading part of the mystery

## The central question

**Some mysteries ask you to find the answer. This one asks what the answer might do to you.**
