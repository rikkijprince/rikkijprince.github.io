*Two epic voyages bordering the impossible.*

## What if the universe's rules lead somewhere stranger than imagination?

Six cosmonauts set out on a journey beyond the Solar System, guided by an android navigator whose memories may not be entirely reliable. Their voyage becomes a test not only of technology and endurance, but of what can be known when distance, time and evidence stretch beyond ordinary experience.

The story then changes perspective. A scientist looks back across an extraordinarily long life, bringing a second kind of uncertainty to the questions raised by the first journey.

## A story at the boundary of science and speculation

*The Edge of Reality* draws on questions from relativity, astrophysics and cosmology while exploring the limits of observation and interpretation. Its two-part structure offers two perspectives on uncertainty: the experience of travelling into the unknown, and the experience of looking back after time has transformed the meaning of a life.

This is speculative fiction for readers who enjoy ideas as much as events—and who want the universe to remain genuinely strange.

## Read it if you enjoy

- Science fiction shaped by cosmology, relativity and the scale of the universe
- Narrators whose memories and testimony cannot simply be taken at face value
- Questions about time, evidence, ageing and the limits of human knowledge
- Stories that respect scientific constraints while exploring imaginative possibilities

## The central question

**The universe has rules. Understanding what they permit may be stranger than breaking them.**
